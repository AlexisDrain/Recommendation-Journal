# Privacy policy: Recommendation Journal

*Last updated: November 13, 2026*

Recommendation Journal is a free, open-source app for saving what people recommend to you. It has
no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive, store,
sell or share any of your data.

## Your list stays on your device

Everything you enter (your recommendations, who recommended them and why, your ratings, notes,
categories and settings) is stored only on your device: in the app on your phone, or in your
browser's own storage if you use the web version. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone
else.

Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) deletes your list. You can export
it at any time as a spreadsheet (.xlsx or .csv), and it is up to you who you share those files with.

## Optional: your own Google Sheet

If you choose to connect a Google Sheet (Settings → Google Sheet), the app sends your list directly
from your device to a script inside **your own** Google Sheet, and reads changes back from it. That
copy is stored in your Google account, under Google's terms and privacy policy. The app never sees
your Google login; it only uses the web address and secret key you paste in. You can disconnect at
any time, and the developer has no access to your sheet.

## What the app connects to, and why

The app works offline. It only goes online for these:

- **App updates (Expo).** To get the newest version of the app, it checks for updates on Expo's
  servers, which handle the app's updates. These checks contain none of your list.
- **New Android versions (GitHub).** The Android app installed from GitHub (not from a store) checks
  the project's GitHub page once a day for a newer version, unless you switch that off in
  Settings → About. This check contains none of your list.
- **Your Google Sheet,** only if you connect one (above).
- **The website** is served by Expo's hosting. Like any website, its servers see the usual request
  details (such as your IP address) when your browser loads the page; your list is not sent.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
